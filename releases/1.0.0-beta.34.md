# 1.0.0-beta.34 — Under the hood: tabs leave the shell, and the housekeeping is done

The last restructuring release. The code behind the tab strips and pane tools (titles, closing,
the tab and pane menus, dragging tabs between panes) moved out of the Desk shell into its own
file, and the arithmetic that sizes panes and seams became a small tested module. That completes
the work that followed the 1 October code review. Nothing should look different.

## What's new

- Nothing visible.

## What to test

Everything about tabs and panes:

- Open several tabs; switch with `Ctrl+Tab` / `Ctrl+Shift+Tab`; close with `Ctrl+W` and with the ×.
- Right-click a tab and try each item (close others, move to the other pane, open to the side,
  Properties). Use a pane's `⋯` menu and its `+` and split buttons.
- Drag a tab to reorder it, to another pane's strip, and onto the edge of a pane to split.
- Make two, three and four panes; drag the seams; right-click a seam and reset it.
- Narrow the window and check panes fold and return; enter and leave Focus mode (`Ctrl+.`).
- Quit and reopen, and check the tabs and split come back.

## Looking back: beta.20 to beta.34

Fifteen releases with almost nothing new to look at. What you got for them:

- **Completing a task no longer stalls saving** (beta.28). The most important fix of the cycle.
- **A failing piece no longer blanks the app** (beta.30), and failed saves and loads now tell
  you instead of failing silently (beta.23).
- **Old databases upgrade** (beta.24); dates are right near midnight (beta.23).
- Status names, phone task grouping, keyboard shortcuts and their tooltips are consistent.
- Screen readers get an editor that announces itself, toasts that are read out and named
  sidebar sections (beta.24).
- Underneath: one automated gate, tests that drive the real app, and a regular health check, so
  the next fifteen releases can be about features.

## Known issues

- With three or four panes, **Hide companion** in a pane's menu can open a second companion; use
  the companion tab's own close button. (Old behaviour, noted in beta.32.)
- The open items from beta.19 still stand: the two-device sync test, and a visual pass.
