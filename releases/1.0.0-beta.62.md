# 1.0.0-beta.62 — A large sample dataset for performance work

Nothing in this release changes what you see. It adds a second, much larger sample dataset to the
tools Hollow is developed with, so that how the app behaves with years of notes and tasks in it
can be measured rather than guessed.

## What's new

- For people working on Hollow's code: the developer-only **Debug** settings can now load
  **SCALE (large vault)** as well as the existing small sample. SCALE is three busy years of
  made-up use: about 3,000 notes (a page for every day, a few very long notes, one folder with
  400 notes in it, folders six levels deep), 4,000 tasks, 40 projects in every state, 12 habits
  with three years of history, and 20 saved layouts. It is the same every time it loads, and its
  dates move with the day you load it. Loading it takes about a minute.

The **Debug** settings exist only in development builds, so the copy of Hollow you test with has
nothing new to see.

## What to test

- Nothing new. Use Hollow as you normally do: open today's page, add and complete a task, keep a
  habit, and check sync still settles if you are signed in.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
