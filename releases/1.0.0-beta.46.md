# 1.0.0-beta.46 — Under the hood: the journal day

The code that decides which day Today shows, finds or creates that day's page, and works out how
far the day arrows can reach has moved out of the large desk shell file into its own piece, with
its own tests. Nothing should look or behave differently.

## What's new

- Nothing you can see. The journal day now has 18 tests of its own.

## What to test

Today and its day arrows, since that is what moved:

- Open Today: today's page is there (or created if this is the first visit today).
- Step back and forward with the day arrows and their keyboard shortcuts. A day you never wrote
  shows as an empty page you can start; it is only created when you write in it.
- Step past the edge of the week: the arrow jumps to the next day that has a page or a task.
- Jump back to today with the Today control.
- Click a date link in a note, and a day in the calendar's "open log": Today opens on that day.
- Leave the app open over midnight if you can: Today moves to the new day.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
