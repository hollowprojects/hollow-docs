# 1.0.0-beta.14 — No more right sidebar

The right sidebar is gone. Everything it showed is now a tab that opens beside the thing it
describes, in the other pane: a task's details, a habit's editor, a note's Properties, and the
day's tasks and habits (the **Day**). Nothing it showed is lost, and none of it is squeezed into a
narrow column any more. The phone app is unchanged.

## What's new

- **Today opens with the Day beside it.** The journal on the left, the day's capture box, habits
  and tasks on the right, as the sidebar's Tasks tab showed them. Close it with the button at the
  far right of the top strip (where the sidebar button was) or **Ctrl+Shift+\\**; Hollow remembers,
  and the next launch opens Today on its own.
- **Details open beside what you clicked.** Click a task in **Planner → List**, a chip in the
  **Calendar**, or a row in a project's **Tasks** tab, and its details open in the pane beside it.
  The list keeps the keyboard, so **↑ / ↓** go on walking rows and the details follow. **Enter**
  opens the selected row. **Habits** works the same way.
- **Clicking into your note no longer swaps the details away.** Before, the sidebar followed
  whichever pane you last clicked. Now each thing stays where you opened it.
- **Room for the editors.** The task editor keeps its dates side by side, and the habit editor its
  labels beside their fields.
- **Properties is a tab too:** **Ctrl+Alt+I**, or right-click a note's tab → **Properties to the
  side**. **Ctrl+I** is still italic.
- **The Day anywhere:** **Ctrl+Shift+1**, or right-click **Today** in the sidebar → **Day to the
  side**.
- **Tabs that follow, and Pin.** A tab that follows your selection is marked **⇄**. Its **Pin**
  button (or right-click the tab → **Pin to what it shows**) keeps it on the task, habit or note it
  shows now.
- **Right-click a task or habit** for **Open detail**, **in new tab**, **to the side** or
  **below**. Ctrl-click opens it in a new tab and Alt-click to the side, as elsewhere.
- **The Pool moved to the List.** The Calendar's Overdue and Pool sidebar is now a **Pool** button
  in the Calendar's header (it shows how many tasks are late) that opens the List beside the
  Calendar, showing only **Overdue** (with **park all →**) and the **Pool**. Each row says why it
  is there. The List has the same **Pool** chip next to its task count. Drag a row onto a Calendar
  day to schedule it.
- **Narrow panes adapt.** A pane under about 400px wide shows the Calendar as its Agenda and List
  rows as just the check, title and date.
- **Focus mode** (**Ctrl+.**) hides the Day or Properties beside your note, as it hides the
  sidebar. Leaving focus mode brings it back.
- **Palette:** **Day**, **Properties** and **Pool**, each **to the side** or **below**.

## What to test

- Launch the app. Today should open with the Day beside it at about three quarters to a quarter.
  Drag the seam, close the Day with **Ctrl+Shift+\\**, restart: Today should open alone. Open the
  Day again and restart: it should come back.
- In **Planner → List**, click a task. Its details open beside it. Press **↓** a few times: the
  details follow. Click into the details, then back into the List: nothing moves.
- With the Day beside Today, right-click a note in the tree → **Open to the side**. The note
  should open as a new tab in the Day's pane, with the Day still there as a tab behind it.
- In a note, press **Ctrl+Alt+I**. Properties opens beside it, and you can keep typing in the note.
  Rename the note from Properties: the title in the note follows.
- Click **Pin** on a companion that follows. The tab should now be named after its task and stop
  following when you pick another task.
- **Calendar → Pool**: the List opens beside it with Overdue and Pool. Try **park all →**, and drag
  a Pool row onto a day.
- Make a pane narrow (drag the seam) with the Calendar in it: it should switch to Agenda, and back
  when widened.
- **Habits → + new habit** should open the new habit's editor beside the list so you can name it.

## Known issues

- The List's **Pool** is not narrowed by the Calendar's project filter the way the old Pool was.
  The **N late** count on the Calendar's Pool button is.
- A companion replaces what was the sidebar's width with a pane, so there are still at most two
  panes. A third document opens as a tab in one of them.
- Two devices editing the same project's milestones or status while one of them is offline can
  lose one of the edits. See the project follow-ups plan.
- The phone's project screens are unchanged, and the phone can't yet start in local mode.
