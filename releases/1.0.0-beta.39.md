# 1.0.0-beta.39 — Under the hood: reopening where you left off

Another piece of the Desk shell moved into its own file: everything about starting up. That is
reading your settings at launch, reopening the tabs and split from last time (or a layout, if
you chose one), and saving the session as you work. Nothing should look different.

## What's new

- Nothing visible.

## What to test

- Open a few tabs and a split, quit and reopen: the same tabs and split should come back.
- In Settings → Desk set **On launch, open** to a layout, quit and reopen: that layout opens.
  Set it back to **Last session** and check your tabs return.
- Change the sidebar size, the split position and Focus-mode choices; quit and reopen; all kept.
- Delete a note that is open in a tab, quit and reopen: that tab is gone and the rest return.

## Known issues

- If the app is very slow to read its settings at launch (several seconds), it can start on a
  single Today tab and then save that over the previous session. Not seen in practice; noted
  for a fix.
- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
