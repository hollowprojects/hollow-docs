# 1.0.0-beta.65 — Signing in on a new computer keeps your data

The first time a computer signs in to your account, Hollow now waits for your data to arrive
before it opens. Until this release it could open on an empty desk first, and what it set up
there was sent back to your account in place of what you had.

## What's new

- **Signing in on a new computer waits for your data.** The first sign-in on a computer shows a
  loading screen while your notes, tasks and settings download; after a moment it says
  **Getting your data. The first sign-in on a computer can take a moment.** Then your desk opens
  as you left it on your other computer.
- **Fixed: a new computer could reset your name and settings.** Signing in on a computer that had
  never had Hollow on it could replace your display name with "User", reset your synced settings
  (density, editor and date preferences), and add a second page for today and a second journal
  folder. It no longer does. If this happened to you on beta.64 or earlier, set your name and
  settings again once, and delete the extra page and folder if you have them.
- **The same fix on the phone.** The phone waits for its first download in the same way. It has
  not been tried on a real phone yet.
- For people working on Hollow's code: the automated smoke check no longer reports a captured
  task as missing when the List happens to be showing only today's tasks; and loading a sample
  dataset into a test account that already holds it no longer stalls sync.

## What to test

- **On a computer that has never had Hollow** (or a fresh copy): sign in to your account. You see
  the loading screen, then your own desk: your name in **Settings**, your settings as you set
  them, one page for today, one journal folder.
- Then on your usual computer: your name and settings are unchanged.
- Sign out and back in on a computer that already has your data: it opens straight away, with no
  loading message.

## Known issues

- If the first download takes longer than a minute (a very slow connection), Hollow opens anyway
  and the old problem can return on that computer. Sign in again on a better connection if your
  name reads "User" afterwards.
- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
