# 1.0.0-beta.57 — Under the hood: the last old names

Hollow's shell was once called the "Notebook". The Desk replaced it, but the old word was still
all through the code that routes you between screens and keeps your history. It is now named
for the Desk, and the last files carrying the old names were renamed. Nothing should look or
behave differently: the styles the app ships are byte-for-byte the same as in beta.56.

## What's new

- Nothing you can see.

## What to test

A general pass, since the rename touched how screens are opened and remembered:

- Start the app: it opens where you left it, with your tabs and pinned projects.
- Open screens from the sidebar and the command palette; step back and forward.
- Open a note, type, and watch the "saved" line under it. Hover a link in a note: its address
  shows in the same line.
- Open a task and change how it repeats.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
