# 1.0.0-beta.40 — Links save before the screen refreshes

Mostly housekeeping, with one fix you may have met.

## What's new

- **Linking and unlinking is reliable.** In a note's Properties panel, linking a note or task,
  or removing a link, told the screen it was finished before the change had been saved. The
  panel then re-read the links and could show the old list until you reopened it. It also meant
  a link that failed (for example linking a note to itself) failed silently. Links are now saved
  first, and a failure shows a message.

## What changed underneath

- The two largest services were split by job. Reading lists of tasks, managing links and
  backlinks, and finding notes by date each have their own service now. Links and backlinks had
  no automated tests before; they have them now.

## What to test

- Open a note's Properties (`Ctrl+Alt+I`). Link another note and a task; each should appear at
  once. Remove each; they should go at once.
- Type a `[[wiki link]]` to another note, save, and check the other note's backlinks list it.
- Today, the Calendar, the List, a project's Tasks tab and search should all show the same
  tasks as before.
- Settings → Writing: change the journal title format and apply it to existing pages.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
