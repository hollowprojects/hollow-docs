# 1.0.0-beta.42 — Under the hood: the desk's panes

The code that lays out the desk's panes, moves focus between them, sizes the seams and decides
which screen a pane shows has moved out of the one large shell file into its own pieces. Nothing
should look or behave differently.

## What's new

- Nothing you can see. The desk shell is about a sixth smaller, and pane focus, splitting and
  seam sizing now have their own tests.

## What to test

Everything about panes, since that is what moved:

- Split the desk and unsplit it with the keyboard shortcut and from the tab strip.
- Move focus between panes with the pane-focus shortcuts, in a two-pane and a four-pane layout.
- Drag the seam between two panes and between two rows; quit and reopen and check the sizes held.
- Drag a tab onto another pane, and onto a pane's edge to split it.
- Open each kind of tab in a pane: Today, List, Calendar, Habits, Projects, a project, a note,
  a template, the activity log, and the companions (a task, a habit, Properties, the day glance).
- Focus mode: turn it on and off, and check the other panes and tab strips hide as your
  settings say.
- Right-click a seam and use its menu.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
