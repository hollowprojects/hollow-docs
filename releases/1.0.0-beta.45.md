# 1.0.0-beta.45 — Your open tabs are safer

Fixes to how Hollow remembers and reopens your desk, and two fixes for desks with several panes.

## What's new

- **A slow or failed start no longer wipes your saved tabs.** If Hollow cannot read your last
  session when it starts, it opens on Today and leaves the saved session alone. Quit and reopen
  to get it back. (The tabs of that one window are not remembered.)
- **Your open tabs are saved sooner.** They are saved as soon as you switch to another app or
  minimise Hollow, not only a second after the last change.
- **Turning off Show Projects moves every open Projects tab to List**, including ones in other
  panes and behind other tabs. Before, only the tab you were looking at moved.
- **"Open in Planner" from the Day panel selects the task in the List it opens**, even when
  another List is already open in a different pane.
- After reopening a session, a failed check for deleted notes or projects no longer closes tabs.

## What to test

- Open several tabs in two panes, switch to another app, then end Hollow from Task Manager.
  Reopen: the tabs should be back.
- Open tabs, quit normally, reopen: same result. Try it with **Settings → Desk → On launch,
  open** set to each choice.
- Delete a note that is open in a tab on another device or in another pane, quit and reopen:
  the dead tab closes and the others stay.
- With two panes, open Planner → Projects in one and something else in the other. Turn off
  **Show Projects** in settings: the Projects tab becomes List.
- With a Planner List open in one pane and the Day panel in another, use a task's
  "open in Planner" in the Day panel: the task is selected in the List that comes forward.

## Known issues

- A change made in the last second before you close the window with its close button may not be
  remembered.
- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
