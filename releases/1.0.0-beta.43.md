# 1.0.0-beta.43 — Under the hood: leftover settings removed

Hollow still carried the plumbing for seven per-device settings that belonged to screens the
Desk replaced long ago (the old task list's collapsed groups and filters, its tab choice, the
old notes drawer, and collapsed habit checklists in the journal). Nothing in the app read or
wrote them any more. They are gone.

## What's new

- Nothing you can see. The app has 14 fewer internal channels and six fewer stored settings.
- The first time this version starts, it removes those six leftover values from the settings
  file on this device. Nothing you set in **Settings** is touched.

## What to test

- Start the app and check your layout, sidebar width, open tabs and theme are as you left them.
- In Planner → List, collapse and expand a group, quit and reopen: it should be as you left it
  (that state was already kept elsewhere and is unaffected).
- On the phone build, the task list's hidden groups still work.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
