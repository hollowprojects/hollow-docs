# 1.0.0-beta.32 — Under the hood: companions leave the shell

Another piece of the Desk shell moved into its own file: companion tabs, the Day, Task, Habit and
Properties panels that open beside what you are working on. Nothing should look different.

## What's new

- Nothing visible.

## What to test

Everything about companions:

- On Today, the Day companion opens beside the note on launch (the Write layout). Close it,
  quit and reopen, and check it stays closed; open it again with `Ctrl+Shift+\`.
- Click a task in the List: its detail opens beside the list. Click a different task: the same
  companion re-targets instead of opening a second one.
- Do the same with a habit in Habits, and with a note's Properties (`Ctrl+Alt+I`).
- `Ctrl+Shift+1` opens the Day beside whatever you are on.
- Drag the seam between a document and its companion, quit and reopen, and check the width is kept.
- Open a task from the Calendar and from a project's Tasks tab.

## Known issues

- With three or four panes, **Hide companion** in a pane's menu can open a second companion (or
  do nothing) when the companion sits in a pane that is not beside the one you are in. Use the
  companion tab's own close button. This is old behaviour, written down now, not new.
- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
