# 1.0.0-beta.9 — A calendar with hours

Planner's Calendar is rebuilt. It now shows tasks on the day they're **due**, has an hour grid for
tasks with a time, and takes tasks dragged in from the List, the Pool and project pages. The List
groups tasks on the same days as the Calendar. You can also drag the line between two side-by-side
panes. The phone app is unchanged apart from the task detail's new Time and Duration fields.

Full guide: **Help → Planner**, **Help → Tabs and the split view** and **Help → Keyboard shortcuts**.

## What's new

### Tasks can have a time

- A task's **due date** can now have a **Time**, and once it has one, a **Duration**. Set both in
  the task's detail. Leave Time empty for an all-day task.
- Task rows show the time next to the due date: "Due Sep 29 · 10:00".
- Times follow **Settings → Time format** (12-hour or 24-hour). That setting was saved before but
  didn't change anything. Now it does.

### The Calendar

- **Day, Week, Month and Agenda.** Day and Week have an all-day row on top and, with **Hours** on,
  an hour grid underneath. A timed task sits at its time, as tall as its duration, and a red line
  marks the current time. Turn **Hours** off to see each day as a column of cards.
- **Today** in the heading jumps back to today. ‹ › step by a day, a week, a month or four weeks,
  depending on the view.
- The Calendar **remembers where you were** while you switch to the List and back, separately in
  each tab.
- **Drag a task onto a day** to make it due that day, or **onto an hour** to give it a time as
  well. Drop it on the **Pool** to unschedule it. **Undo** appears for a few seconds after each
  move.
- **Drag in from anywhere.** List rows and a project's open tasks can be dragged onto the Calendar,
  including across a split view.
- A task that only has a **do date** shows on that day with a dashed outline: it's planned, not
  due. Dragging it moves the do date, so nothing gets a deadline you didn't set. A do date that has
  passed without the task being done moves up to today.
- **Keyboard:** with a task focused, **Alt+← / Alt+→** moves it a day, **Alt+↑ / Alt+↓** moves it
  an hour, and **Delete** unschedules it.
- **Click a task** to open its detail in the right panel, under the Pool. You stay in the Calendar.
- **Add a task** from the **+** in a day's all-day row, or double-click an hour.
- **Month:** **+N more** opens that day. **Agenda** shows the next four weeks and skips empty days.
- **Removed:** the undated "this week / this month" tray, the week bars in Month, and the "N planned
  · M in pool" count. Tasks planned for a week or month with no day are now in the Pool, labelled
  "this week" or "this month".

### The List

- Date groups are now **Overdue**, **Today**, **Next 7 days**, **Later**, **Unscheduled** and
  **Done**. A task sits on the same day here as it does in the Calendar.
- The date column shows the due day and time ("Today 10:00", "Wed", "Sep 25"). Its menu sets the
  due date and has **Time…**. A task with only a do date shows it faintly, and its menu still moves
  the do date.
- Drag a row onto the **Unscheduled** heading to clear its due date.

### Split view

- **Drag the line between two panes** to give one more room. Double-click it, or press Enter while
  it has focus, to go back to half and half. Arrow keys move it too. Side-by-side and stacked each
  remember their own position.

## What to test

- Give a task a time and a duration in its detail. Check it shows in the Calendar's hour grid, on
  the task's row in Today and in the List. Switch **Settings → Time format** and check all three
  follow.
- Open List and Calendar side by side. Drag a List row onto a day, then onto an hour, then back onto
  the Pool. Check the List regroups each time and **Undo** puts it back.
- Drag a task from a project's page onto the Calendar.
- Focus a task in the Calendar and use Alt+arrows and Delete. Check that Alt+← moves the task and
  doesn't take you back a page.
- Switch to the List and back, then open a new tab, and check the Calendar kept its place in each.
- From Today's task menu, choose **Show in Calendar to the side**. It should open on that day.
- Drag the line between two panes, restart the app, split again, and check the position is kept.
  Try it stacked too.
- Sign in on a second device and check a task's time syncs both ways.

## Known issues

- **Older versions don't know about task times.** On a device still running beta.8, a time you set
  won't show. If that device clears the task's due date, the time stays stored but is ignored.
- With **Planner in both panes**, clicking a Planner row in the sidebar switches the first pane,
  not the one you're working in.
- The project Board's own date-and-time field sets when you plan to *do* the task. It doesn't show
  on the Calendar's hour grid.
- The phone's task list still uses the old groups (Late, Today, Upcoming…).
- The `{{time}}` template variable always uses 24-hour time.
