# 1.0.0-beta.7 — The sidebar, as an accordion

The left sidebar has a new shape. Instead of one long list behind a **Notes | Planner** switch, it
now has **Today** pinned at the top and three sections below it — **Planner**, **Projects** and
**Notes**. One section is open at a time and takes all the room that is left, so its headings never
scroll away however many notes or projects you have.

Full guide: **Help → The sidebar**.

## What's new

### One open section, always

- **Today** sits above the sections and is always one click away.
- Click a closed section heading to open it; the one that was open closes. Clicking the heading of
  the section that is already open does nothing, so the sidebar is never empty.
- The open section scrolls on its own. The headings and the foot of the sidebar stay put.
- Which section you left open is remembered, on each device separately.
- The **Notes | Planner** switch at the top of the sidebar is gone — the sections replaced it.

### Planner

- **List**, **Calendar** and **Habits** each have a row. The current week number sits beside
  Calendar.
- Projects is no longer a row here — it has a section of its own. It is still Planner's fourth tab.

### Projects

- Projects are grouped by their state: **Active**, **On ice** and **Done**. Done is collapsed
  behind a count until you open it, and stays how you leave it.
- The heading counts your **active** projects, so the number is work in hand rather than history.
- **Pinned projects sort to the top of their group.** Pinning no longer decides whether a project
  is listed — every project is in the sidebar now, which is new: before, pinning anything hid all
  the others.
- The small "on ice" and "done" badges on each row are gone; the group heading says it instead.
- The section ends with **All projects**, which opens the Projects tab. The old **All projects**
  button at the foot of the sidebar is gone.

### Notes

- The note explorer is unchanged, and keeps its `+` button for a new note, folder or project.
- The heading counts the notes you can see — archived ones are not counted.
- Folders now use a **chevron** that turns as they open, matching the section headings. They used
  to be small circles.

### Marks for what is already open

- A row that is open in another tab or the other pane is marked `OPEN`. Clicking it switches to
  that tab rather than opening a second copy. This worked on the phone already; it now works on the
  desktop too, on Planner rows, project rows and notes in the explorer.
- The row for whatever you are looking at is highlighted instead.

### Small things

- **Ctrl+N** (**⌘N**) makes a new note wherever you are. It used to work only while the sidebar was
  showing the note explorer.
- Opening something from search, a link or a tab no longer swings the sidebar to a different list.
  It only moves the highlight, so the section you chose stays open.

## On the phone

The drawer on the left is the same sidebar, so it gets the same accordion, sized for a finger.
Two things are **removed** from it in this release:

- **The projects list is gone from the drawer**, along with the Planner **Projects** row. Neither
  led anywhere yet — there is no project screen on the phone — so both opened a placeholder. They
  come back with the screen.
- Projects are still reachable on the phone through search.

The Planner **Calendar** row stays, and still opens a placeholder, because a calendar is a view
people go looking for and it is coming sooner.

## What to test

- Open each section and check the one you left open is still open after a restart.
- With a long note tree, check the **Planner** and **Projects** headings stay visible.
- Make a project on ice and one done, and check they group correctly and that Done stays collapsed
  or expanded as you left it.
- Pin a project and check it rises to the top of its group.
- Open the same note in two tabs and check the `OPEN` mark and that clicking it switches tabs.
- Press **Ctrl+N** with the Planner section open.
- On a short window (under about 520px tall), check the sidebar scrolls rather than crushing the
  open section.

## Known issues

- Opening something from outside the sidebar does not open the section it lives in. If the row is
  inside a closed section you will not see it highlighted. This is deliberate for now.
- Reordering projects or folders by dragging them in the sidebar is still not possible.
