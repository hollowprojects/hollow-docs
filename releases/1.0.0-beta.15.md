# 1.0.0-beta.15 — Up to four panes, and layouts you can keep

The main area now holds up to four panes, Hollow reopens the tabs you left open, and you can save
an arrangement of panes under a name and bring it back in one click — on any of your computers.
The phone app is unchanged.

## What's new

- **Up to four panes.** The **Layout** button in the top strip (where the split button was) offers
  **One pane**, **Side by side**, **Stacked**, **Three panes** (one tall, two stacked) and **Four
  panes** (two by two). Its icon shows the layout you're in. **Ctrl+Alt+\\** still splits one pane
  in two, or folds everything back into one.
- **Each side of a four-pane layout resizes on its own.** Drag the line between two panes; the left
  and right sides each have their own.
- **Move between panes from the keyboard** with **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+← → ↑ ↓**. (Plain
  **Ctrl+Alt+← / →** still steps the day on Today.)
- **Move to ▸.** With more than two panes, right-click a tab → **Move to** and pick a pane by where
  it sits, such as **Top right pane**.
- **Small windows fold, they don't rearrange.** When the window is too small for your layout,
  panes stack or shrink to their tab strip, and clicking a strip opens it. Make the window bigger
  and the layout is back exactly as it was.
- **Hollow reopens your tabs.** Quit with a split and a few tabs open, and the next launch brings
  them back: the same panes, tabs, Planner views, Calendar project filters and seam positions.
  *Today* comes back as today, not the day you quit on. Tabs whose note, project, task or habit was
  deleted are left out. Turn it off in **Settings → Modules → Reopen tabs on launch**.
- **Saved layouts.** **Layout → Save current layout…** keeps the panes and tabs you have now under
  a name, like *Morning*. Your saved layouts are listed in the same menu and in the command
  palette (**Layout: Morning**), with **Rename layout** and **Delete layout** (with Undo). Saving
  again under the same name updates it.
- **Saved layouts sync.** Save one on your desk computer and it shows up on your laptop. If the
  laptop's window is smaller, the layout folds to fit instead of breaking. A tab for a note that
  isn't on that computer is left out and the rest opens.

## What to test

- **Layouts.** From one pane, pick each of the five layouts in turn. New panes should copy the tab
  you were on, and your left pane should stay on the left. Go back to **One pane**: no tab should
  be lost, and a tab open twice should be kept once.
- **Four panes, then resize.** Drag the line between the columns, then each side's own line. Each
  should move on its own. Double-click a line: it goes back to half and half.
- **Keyboard.** In four panes, **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+→** then **↓**: focus should move right, then down.
  On Today, plain **Ctrl+Alt+→** should still step to the next day.
- **Small window.** In four panes, make the window narrow and short. The panes should fold and
  show strips; click a strip to open it. Make the window big again: the four panes come back.
- **Reopen.** Quit with three tabs and a split open, then launch again: the same tabs and split
  come back, and Today shows today. Then delete a note that was open in a tab, quit and launch: that
  tab is gone and nothing complains. Turn **Reopen tabs on launch** off: launch starts on Today.
- **Saved layouts.** Set up Today | Day on the left and List | Calendar on the right. **Layout →
  Save current layout…**, name it *Morning*. Switch to **One pane**, then pick *Morning* from the
  **Layout** menu, and again from the palette (type *morning*). It should come back exactly.
  Rename it, delete it, then **Undo**.
- **On two computers (signed in).** Save a layout on one; open the **Layout** menu on the other after
  it syncs, and the layout should be there. Rename a *different* layout on each computer at the same
  time: both renames should stick.

## Known issues

- Two Today panes always show the same day, and two Planner panes share one selected day.
- Four live screens is the most Hollow has shown at once; if it feels slow on your machine, say so
  in a bug report.
- Two devices editing the same project's milestones or status while one of them is offline can
  lose one of the edits. See the project follow-ups plan.
- The phone's project screens are unchanged, and the phone can't yet start in local mode.
