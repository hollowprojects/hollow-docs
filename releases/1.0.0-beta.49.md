# 1.0.0-beta.49 — Under the hood: old names retired

Some of Hollow's code still lived under the names of screens the Desk replaced long ago. The
notes tree, the task editor and a few note views shared one folder with a retired name; they are
now sorted into folders named for what they are, and the old name is gone from the task editor
and its helpers. Nothing should look or behave differently: the styles the app ships are
byte-for-byte the same as in beta.48.

## What's new

- Nothing you can see.

## What to test

A quick look at the three things that moved:

- The notes tree in the sidebar: open, rename, drag and right-click a note.
- The task editor: open a task from the List and from the Calendar; change its date, its
  repeat, its steps and its notes; do the same for a habit.
- A note's Outline and Connections panels.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
