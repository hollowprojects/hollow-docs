# 1.0.0-beta.12 — Undo and quick-add fixes

Three fixes to things beta.11 added. Nothing new to learn. The phone app is unchanged.

## What's fixed

- **Typing `!1`, `!2` or `!3` works anywhere in a new task.** In beta.11 it only worked at the very
  end — `!1 Ship it` or `Call mom !2 soon` kept the `!1` in the title and set no priority.
- **Undo after dragging a repeating task out of Done** no longer creates an extra copy of its next
  occurrence. It goes back to Done with today as its completion date.
- **Undo after dragging a task to another milestone** keeps anything you changed on that task in
  the meantime (a snooze, a repeat setting). Before, Undo put those back as they were at the drag.

## What to test

- Add tasks with `!1 Ship it`, `Call mom !2 soon` and `Tidy up !3`. Each should get its priority and
  lose the `!n` from its title.
- Make a task repeat (for example every 7 days), tick it, then in the List grouped by Status drag it
  from Done into Doing and press **Undo**. It should be back in Done, and there should be only one
  upcoming copy of it.
- On a project's Tasks tab grouped by Milestone, drag a task to another milestone, snooze it from its
  detail, then **Undo** the drag. The task goes back to its old milestone and stays snoozed.

## Known issues

- Two devices editing the same project's milestones or status while one of them is offline can
  lose one of the edits. Still waiting on a two-device test — see the project follow-ups plan.
- The phone's project screens are unchanged.
