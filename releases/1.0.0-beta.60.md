# 1.0.0-beta.60 — A Today dashboard, Focus mode for any pane, and layouts that do more

## What's new

- **The Today dashboard.** **Today** at the top of the sidebar now opens a dashboard: today's
  tasks with checkboxes, what is next at a set time, what is overdue, your habits, projects with
  something due this week, the first lines of today's page, and a bar for each of the next seven
  days. Each tile's heading opens the real screen. A line at the top adds a task for today.
  Today's page itself is still `Ctrl+Shift+T`, and **Today's page** in the row's right-click menu.
- **Focus mode works on any pane.** `Ctrl+.` makes the pane you are in fill the window — a note,
  the List, the Calendar, a project. Leave with `Ctrl+.`, `Esc`, or **Exit focus** (top right of
  the pane when it is not a note). While it is on, messages that need nothing from you wait;
  errors and **Undo** still show. **Other panes** is gone from **Settings → Desk → Focus mode
  hides**, because the other panes now always go.
- **A layout can open in Focus mode.** Save a layout while Focus mode is on, and it opens in it.
- **Back to what I had.** After opening a layout, **Back to what I had** in the **Layout** menu
  puts the screen you replaced back, once.
- **"The project in front".** When you save a layout with a project in a pane, choose **The
  project in front**, and the layout opens on whichever project you were last looking at.
- **The List's Today filter.** A **Today** chip beside **Pool** narrows the List to overdue work
  and today's. **Daily log** (`Ctrl+1`) now shows it beside today's page, in place of the Day.
- **Calendar and List side by side work together.** Click a day's heading in the Calendar and the
  List beside it narrows to that day; its date chip puts the whole List back.
- **Bug reports in local mode** are copied to the clipboard as well as saved in **Beta
  feedback**, ready to paste.

## What to test

- Click **Today** in the sidebar. Tick a task on the dashboard, then open the List: it is done
  there too. Add a task from the line at the top; it appears under **Today**. Click **Journal**:
  today's page opens. Click a day in **Next seven days**: the Calendar opens on it.
- Open the List and press `Ctrl+.`: it fills the window, with **Exit focus** at the top right.
  Press `Esc`. Split into two panes and do the same from each.
- In Focus mode, complete a task (no "done" message should appear) and delete one (the **Undo**
  message still appears).
- Press `Ctrl+1` from a desk of your own, then **Layout ▾ → Back to what I had**: your tabs come
  back. The item is greyed out again afterwards.
- Open a project, **Save as new layout…**, choose **The project in front**, save it on a key.
  Open a different project, press that key: the layout opens on the second project.
- Turn on Focus mode, save a layout, leave Focus mode, open the layout: Focus mode is on.
- Put a Calendar beside a List (**Layout ▾ → Side by side**). Click a day's heading in the
  Calendar: the List shows only that day, with a date chip. Click the chip.
- In local mode, send a bug report: it is saved as a note, and pasting shows it as markdown.

## Known issues

- If you had **Other panes** unticked in **Focus mode hides**, Focus mode now hides the other
  panes anyway; that is the change.
- The dashboard's **Projects this week** counts due dates only, not milestone dates.
- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
