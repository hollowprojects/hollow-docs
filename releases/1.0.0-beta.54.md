# 1.0.0-beta.54 — Under the hood: the sidebar's state

The code that keeps the sidebar's state (full, icons or hidden; its width; pinned projects; the
task counts beside each view and project) has moved out of the large desk shell file into its
own piece, with its own tests. Nothing should look or behave differently.

## What's new

- Nothing you can see. The sidebar's state handling now has 15 tests of its own.

## What to test

The sidebar, since that is what moved:

- Step it between full, icons and hidden with the top-bar control, and hide and show it with
  its keyboard shortcut. Restart: it comes back as you left it.
- Drag its edge to resize it. Restart: the width held.
- Pin and unpin a project. Archive a pinned project: it leaves the pins.
- The counts beside Today and beside each project follow as you add and complete tasks.
- Narrow the window until the sidebar draws as icons, then widen it again.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
