# 1.0.0-beta.51 — Under the hood: tests that look at the screen the way you do

More of Hollow's automated tests now find things the way a person or a screen reader does (by
role and name) instead of by internal style names. Two small accessibility fixes came out of it.

## What's new

- **Editor toolbar:** the button clusters are announced as named groups: "Text formatting",
  "Headings and quotes", "Lists", "Insert", "Template variables", "Properties", "Editor mode".
  The names were there before but were never read out.
- **A project's Activity tab:** each row's coloured dot is read as its kind ("task", "note" or
  "project") before the row's text.
- Nothing looks different on screen.

## What to test

- Open a note and use the formatting toolbar: every button works as before.
- Open a project's Activity tab: rows look as before.
- With a screen reader, move through the editor toolbar and listen for the group names.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
