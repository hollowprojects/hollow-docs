# 1.0.0-beta.48 — Accessibility: names, groups and announcements

A pass over the places where a screen reader had nothing to hold on to. Nothing looks different
on screen.

## What's new

- **The rail reads properly.** Rows say "Today, 4 tasks open", "Calendar, week 39", or
  "Thesis, open in another tab, 2 today, 1 overdue" instead of words and numbers run together.
  The four views are a group called "Views".
- **The notes tree has named sections.** A pinned project says "pinned to the rail", a project's
  own page says "project page", a linked note says "linked", and a note open in another tab
  says so.
- **Tasks.** In the day glance every tick says which task, habit or step it completes. In the
  task list each group (Overdue, Today, a milestone) is announced by name, and a late task's
  date says "late".
- **Calendar.** The date range is a heading and is announced when you move to another week, day
  or month. Week, Day and Month are grids with weekday headers, and every day is named with its
  weekday and full date in your Date format. Under a project filter, items from outside the
  project say "(outside the project filter)".
- **Projects.** Each row in the Projects list reads as a sentence: name, status, progress, next
  due, open and overdue counts. In **Edit project**, clicking a label focuses its field.
- **Announcements.** Entering and leaving Focus mode, the drop target while dragging a tab or a
  task, and loading spinners are now announced.
- **Top bar.** The Layout button says which key the open layout is on.
- **Phone.** The bottom bar and the drawer, and the two formatting toolbars, have different
  names.

## What to test

- Look over the rail, the notes tree, Planner (List, Calendar in Week, Day and Month), the
  Projects list, a project page and **Edit project**: nothing should have moved, changed size or
  changed colour. The Calendar's Month and Week grids are the most likely place for a layout
  slip.
- If you use a screen reader (Narrator: `Win+Ctrl+Enter`), walk the rail and the Planner and
  check the names above are what you hear.
- Drag a task between groups in the List and onto a day in the Calendar; drag a tab onto
  another pane. All should work as before.

## Known issues

- In the Calendar you cannot yet move between days with the arrow keys; `Tab` reaches
  everything.
- The notes tree does not yet announce nesting level.
- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
