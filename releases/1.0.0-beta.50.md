# 1.0.0-beta.50 — Under the hood: one door for per-device settings

Every per-device setting (rail width, open tabs, layout slots, interface size and so on) used to
have its own pair of internal channels. They now all go through one typed pair, and fifteen more
leftover settings that no screen read were removed. Nothing should look or behave differently.

## What's new

- Nothing you can see. The app has 64 fewer internal channels than beta.49 and stores 15 fewer
  leftover values.
- The first time this version starts, it removes those leftover values from the settings file
  on this device. Nothing you set in **Settings** is touched.

## What to test

Everything Hollow remembers on this device, since all of it moved:

- Start the app: the window opens at your interface size, on the screen and day you left.
- **Settings → Appearance → Interface size**: change it; the app resizes and the choice holds
  after a restart.
- The rail: step it between full, icons and hidden; drag its width; restart and check both held.
- Open a layout with `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+6`, drag a seam between panes, hide and show the
  companion, then restart: tabs, sizes and the companion come back.
- **Settings → Desk**: the "On launch, open" choice, what Focus mode hides, and Show Projects.
- The notes tree: which folders and journal months are open, the sort order and pinned projects
  survive a restart.
- **Settings → Writing**: the journal title format saves, and "Apply to existing" still works.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
