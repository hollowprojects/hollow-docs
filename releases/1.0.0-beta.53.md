# 1.0.0-beta.53 — More labels follow your Date and Time format

## What's new

- **Habit squares name their day in your Date format.** In the Habits grid, the habit editor's
  five-week grid, the Calendar's habit chips and the phone's grid, the tooltip (and what a
  screen reader says) used to read `2026-07-06` whatever you had chosen.
- **A note's Created and Updated tooltip** shows the exact time in your Time format.
- **Bug reports:** the times beside errors in the "attached context" preview follow your Time
  format. What is sent with the report is unchanged.
- Under the hood: six internal channels that nothing called, and the code behind them, were
  removed.

## What to test

- Change **Settings → Date format**, then hover a habit's squares in Planner → Habits, in the
  habit editor, and on a habit chip in the Calendar: the date follows the setting.
- Change **Settings → Time format**, then hover a note's Created / Updated in Connections.
- Complete a task and a habit, and write in a note: all as before. Your habit streaks are
  unchanged.
- Open **Report a bug** and look at the attached context.

## Known issues

- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
