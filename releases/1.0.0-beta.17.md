# 1.0.0-beta.17 — Layouts on Ctrl+1 to 6, and a flat sidebar

Two changes to how you get around. Saved layouts now sit on **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+6**, with four
ready-made ones to start from, and the **Layout** button tells you which one you are in and whether
you have changed it. The sidebar no longer opens one section at a time: Today, List, Calendar,
Habits, your projects and your notes are all on show at once, and it can shrink to a column of
icons. The phone app gets the flat sidebar in its drawer; nothing else changes there.

## Read this first: what moved

- **Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2 now open layouts.** They used to go to Today and the Planner. Now **Ctrl+1**
  opens **Write** (Today with the Day beside it) and **Ctrl+2** opens **Plan** (Calendar with the
  List beside it).
- **Opening a layout replaces the tabs you have open.** Every pane and tab on screen is swapped for
  the layout's. Your notes are saved first, and nothing is deleted, but the tabs are gone. If you
  want to keep an arrangement, save it as a layout first.
- **Today is Ctrl+Shift+T.** Press it again while on Today to jump back to today's date, as a
  second **Ctrl+1** used to. The Planner has no key of its own any more: use **List**,
  **Calendar** and **Habits** in the sidebar, or the command palette.
- **The sidebar's sections no longer open one at a time.** Everything is on show at once.
- **Projects that are on ice or done are no longer listed in the sidebar.** It shows your active
  projects, five at most. Click the **Projects** heading or **All projects** for the rest.
- **The sync status moved** from the top strip to the foot of the sidebar.
- **The companion button left the top strip.** Use **Ctrl+Shift+\\**, **Show companion** /
  **Hide companion** in the **⋯** menu at the end of a pane's tab strip, or **Toggle companion** in
  the command palette.

## What's new

### Layouts

- **Six shortcuts.** **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+6** each open a layout. The **Layout** menu lists the six
  first, with the key beside each name, and a ✓ on the one you are in.
- **Four layouts come with Hollow**, on **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+4**. **Ctrl+5** and **Ctrl+6** start
  empty.
  - **Write** — Today, with the Day beside it.
  - **Plan** — the Calendar, with the List beside it showing the **Pool**: overdue tasks, then
    tasks with no day yet.
  - **Research** — whatever tab you are on, with its **Properties** beside it.
  - **Review** — four panes: Today and the Day above, the List and the Calendar below.
- **An empty shortcut offers to save.** Press the key of an empty one, or click **Empty slot** in
  the menu, and the **Save layout** box opens with that key already chosen.
- **The Layout button names the layout you are in** and shows its key, such as **Ctrl+4**. A **dot**
  appears when the screen no longer matches what was saved: you added or closed a pane, opened or
  closed a tab, changed a Planner tab's view, or dragged a line between panes. Switching between
  tabs, clicking into another pane, reordering tabs and focus mode do not count.
- **Update “name”** in the **Layout** menu saves the screen as it is now into the layout you are
  in. It is greyed out, with **No changes**, when there is nothing to save. Updating one of the four
  that come with Hollow saves your own copy under the same name and gives it the key.
- **Save as new layout…** (**Ctrl+Alt+S**) replaces **Save current layout…**. It asks for:
  - a name;
  - a **Shortcut**: **1** to **6**, each showing what it holds now, or **No shortcut**. Taking a
    key that is in use only takes the key; the layout that had it is kept;
  - for a pane showing a daily note that has been written, **Today’s page** or **This page**:
    open on whatever day it is, or always on that exact page.

  Saving under a name you already use still replaces that layout.
- **Manage layouts…** lists every layout with a short description (*Today | Day*), a shortcut
  dropdown, and **Rename** and **Delete** for the ones you saved. **Delete** still has **Undo**.
  **Restore defaults** puts Write, Plan, Research and Review back on **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+4** and
  deletes nothing. **Rename layout** and **Delete layout** are no longer in the **Layout** menu;
  they are here.
- **Layouts keep the position of every line between panes.** A layout saved at 70 / 30 comes back
  at 70 / 30. Right-click a line for **Reset to “name”**, which puts the lines back where the
  layout has them.
- **Hollow remembers the layout across a restart.** With **Reopen tabs on launch** on, the
  **Layout** button names the layout again after you relaunch.
- **A layout that names something deleted still opens.** The missing tab is left out and a message
  says so. The saved layout is not changed.
- **Command palette.** **Layout:** rows now show the key, such as **Layout: Write (Ctrl+1)**, and
  include the four that come with Hollow.

### Panes and tabs

- **Drag a tab to a pane's right or bottom edge to split there.** The half it will take lights up.
  The right edge works while there is one column; the bottom edge works while that side has one
  pane.
- **Three buttons at the end of every tab strip**, acting on that pane:
  - **+** (**New tab**): Today, List, Calendar, Habits, Projects or **New note**.
  - The split button: **Split right** or **Split down**. A direction is greyed out when there is
    no room for it.
  - **⋯** (**Pane menu**): **Move all tabs to**, **Show companion** / **Hide companion**, **Close
    other tabs**, and **Close pane**, which keeps the pane's tabs by moving them next door.
- **Right-click the line between two panes** for **Swap panes**, **Equalise** (all panes the same
  size) and **Close left pane** / **Close right pane** (or **upper** / **lower**).
- **Day to the side** is in a tab's right-click menu.

### Sidebar and top strip

- **A flat sidebar.** **Today**, **List**, **Calendar** and **Habits** are always at the top. Below
  them are **Projects** (your active ones, pinned first, five at most, then **All projects**) and
  **Notes**, which takes the rest of the height. The small arrow by **Projects** folds its rows
  away.
- **An icon sidebar.** The sidebar button next to the back and forward arrows now has three stops:
  full, a narrow column of icons, and hidden. In the icon column, **Projects** and **Notes** open a
  panel beside it. The panel closes when you open something; click its pin (**Keep open**) and it
  stays, and the panes move over to make room. **Ctrl+\\** still hides and shows the sidebar,
  bringing it back the way you last had it.
- **In a narrow window** (under about 1100 pixels wide) a full sidebar is shown as icons, and comes
  back when you widen the window.
- **Layout is the one control at the top right**, next to the window buttons.
- **The sync status is at the foot of the sidebar** and is always shown while you are signed in:
  **Synced** when there is nothing to report, otherwise a word such as **Syncing…** or
  **Offline**. In the icon sidebar it is a dot. Click it for **Settings → About**.
- **New in the command palette:** **Layout: One pane**, **Side by side**, **Stacked**, **Three
  panes** and **Four panes**; **Rail: Show**, **Rail: Icons only** and **Rail: Hide**; and **Toggle
  companion**.

## What to test

- **The four layouts.** Press **Ctrl+1**, **Ctrl+2**, **Ctrl+3** and **Ctrl+4** in turn. Each
  should replace the screen with its layout, and the **Layout** button should show its name and key.
  For **Ctrl+3**, open a note first: it should stay on the left with Properties beside it.
- **The dot.** In **Write**, drag the line between the panes: the dot appears. Right-click the line
  → **Reset to “Write”**: the dot goes. Switch tabs and click between panes: no dot. Open a new tab:
  the dot appears.
- **Save to an empty shortcut.** Arrange two panes your own way and press **Ctrl+5**. The **Save
  layout** box opens with **5** chosen. Name it and save. Press **Ctrl+1**, then **Ctrl+5**: your
  arrangement comes back, with the line where you left it.
- **Update.** In your layout, add a tab, then **Layout → Update “name”**. Go to another layout and
  back: the new tab is there. Do the same in **Write**: afterwards **Manage layouts…** should show
  a Write of your own on **Ctrl+1**.
- **Today’s page / This page.** On Today, with something written on the page, choose **Save as
  new layout…** and pick **This page** for that pane. Tomorrow, the layout should open on that
  same page, not on the new day. Saved with **Today’s page**, it should open on the new day.
- **Take a key that is in use.** Save a layout onto **2**. **Plan** should lose the key, not be
  replaced. **Manage layouts… → Restore defaults** should put Plan back on **Ctrl+2** and keep your
  layout, now without a key.
- **Rename and delete** in **Manage layouts…**. After **Delete**, press **Undo**: the layout
  should come back on its key.
- **Something deleted.** Save a layout with a note open in it, delete the note, then open the
  layout: the rest opens and a message says a tab was left out.
- **Restart.** Quit in a layout and relaunch: the **Layout** button should name it again.
- **Drag to split.** With one pane and three tabs, drag a tab onto the right edge of the pane,
  then another onto the bottom edge of the pane it came from.
- **Sidebar.** Click the sidebar button three times: icons, hidden, full. In icons, open **Notes**,
  open a note (the panel closes), then pin the panel and open two more (it stays). Make the window
  narrow: the full sidebar becomes icons, and comes back when you widen it.
- **Pane buttons and the line menu.** Try **+**, the split button and **⋯** on each pane of a
  four-pane layout, then right-click each line: **Swap panes**, **Equalise**, **Close … pane**.
- **Keys.** **Ctrl+Shift+T** from the Planner goes to Today; pressed again after stepping back a
  day, it returns to today. **Ctrl+Alt+S** opens **Save layout**.
- **On two computers (signed in).** Save a layout on one. On the other it should appear in the
  **Layout** menu below the six shortcuts, with no key, until you give it one in **Manage
  layouts…**.

## Known issues

- **A shortcut belongs to one computer.** A saved layout syncs; the key it sits on does not. On
  your other computer the layout shows without a key until you give it one there.
- **One of the four that come with Hollow, once taken off its key, is not listed anywhere.**
  **Manage layouts… → Restore defaults** brings all four back.
- **Research, once updated, opens a fixed tab.** Your copy opens the tab that was on the left when
  you updated it, not whatever tab you are on. Its Properties pane still follows.
- Layouts cannot be reordered; **Manage layouts…** lists them by shortcut, then the rest.
- A layout does not remember focus mode, back and forward history, or the Calendar's day.
- The phone has no sync status in its drawer, and no layouts.
- Two Today panes always show the same day, and two Planner panes share one selected day.
- Two devices editing the same project's milestones or status while one of them is offline can
  lose one of the edits. See the project follow-ups plan.
- The phone's project screens are unchanged, and the phone can't yet start in local mode.
