# 1.0.0-beta.35 — The right day, wherever you are

A fix for anyone east of Greenwich (Europe, Africa, Asia, Australia), and two smaller ones.

## What's new

- **Habit ticks and "today" land on the right day east of Greenwich.** In a few places Hollow
  worked out "today" by a route that gives the date in London, not the date where you are. West
  of London that happens to be right. East of it, it is yesterday, all day. A habit ticked
  without choosing a day, a quick task's date, and the window used to count a streak could all
  land a day early. They now use your local date. Days already recorded are not changed.
- **A task template's repeat setting can be cleared.** Editing a template and removing its
  "repeats" setting did not stick; it does now.
- **Templates are checked against their owner** before they are changed or deleted. With one
  profile on a device this changes nothing you can see.

## What to test

- If you are east of London: tick a habit for today (from Habits and from the Day companion) and
  check it shows on today, not yesterday. Add a quick task and check it appears under today.
- Anywhere: late in the evening, tick a habit and check it is on today.
- Edit a task template, clear its repeat setting, save, reopen it and check it stayed cleared.
- Create, edit and delete a note template and a task template; both should work as before.

## Known issues

- A due date is still stored in a way that can shift by a day if you open the app in a different
  time zone from the one where you set it. That is a larger change, planned, and waiting on the
  two-device sync test.
- The open items from beta.19 still stand.
