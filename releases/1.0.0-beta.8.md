# 1.0.0-beta.8 — More room to write

A polish pass on the desktop app. The sidebar gives more room to your notes and projects, Planner
drops a row of repeated buttons, and the page you write on is narrower, quieter, and can take over
the whole window. On the phone, the only change is that you can close the open section of the left
drawer.

Full guide: **Help → The sidebar**, **Help → Notes** and **Help → Keyboard shortcuts**.

## What's new

### The sidebar

- **You can close the open section.** Click the heading of the open section and it closes, leaving
  all three headings stacked at the top. Click any heading to open it again. The app remembers
  whether you left a section open, including none. The phone's left drawer works the same way.
- **The foot of the sidebar is one row of icons**: **Help**, **Report a bug** and **Settings** on
  the left, and your account on the right. Hover over an icon to see its name and shortcut. The
  shortcuts are unchanged (F1, Ctrl+Shift+B, Ctrl+,). This gives the open section about three more
  rows. When you're working offline, a small dot on the account picture says so. Click it to sign
  in.
- **Journal days in the tree show their date**, for example `09/26/2026`, instead of a long title
  that got cut off. They follow the date format in **Settings**. Hover over a row to see the page's
  full title. The day you're viewing is highlighted.

### Planner

- The **List | Calendar | Habits | Projects** buttons at the top of Planner are gone. Switch views
  from the sidebar's Planner rows, which already open in a new tab (Ctrl+click) or beside the
  current one (Alt+click). If the sidebar is hidden, press **Ctrl+\\** to show it.
- Each view's heading is now its own name: **List**, **Calendar**, **Habits** or **Projects**.
- Calendar's controls (dates, ‹ ›, Week / Month / Agenda, Habits and the planned count) have moved
  into the heading row, so the grid starts higher. In a narrow pane the planned count wraps onto its
  own line.
- A project's page no longer has Planner's buttons above it.

### Writing

- **The text column is narrower**, about 66 characters wide, whatever the window size. Long pages
  are easier to read. This applies to notes and to project pages.
- **The formatting toolbar stays out of sight until you need it.** It appears when you point at the
  page or start typing, and the page doesn't move when it does.
- **A line under the page** shows the word count, the reading time and whether your changes are
  saved. **Properties → Stats** uses the same numbers.
- Note titles are bigger.
- **Focus mode**: click **Focus** at the top right of a note, or press **Ctrl+.**. It hides the
  sidebar, the right panel and the tabs. Press **Esc**, Ctrl+. again, or **Exit focus** to bring
  them back exactly as they were. Leaving the note for another screen also ends focus mode.

### Moving between days

- The day heading is now at the left: **‹**, the date, **›**, then **Today**.
- **Click the date to open a month calendar.** Days with an entry have a dot, and the heading
  counts them. Pick a day to go to it. Faded days are too far from anything written to open.
- **Ctrl+Alt+← / Ctrl+Alt+→** step one day back or forward.
- **Today** is a label when you're on today, and a button when you've stepped away. **Ctrl+1**
  also brings you back: press it once to get to the Today tab, and press it again to jump to
  today's date.
- A day with nothing written now says so: "Nothing written on this day." Click the page to start
  the entry.

## What to test

- Close the open sidebar section, restart the app, and check it's still closed.
- Hover over each footer icon, then click it. Do the same with the account picture, both signed in
  and offline.
- Change the date format in **Settings** and check the journal rows in the tree follow it.
- In Planner, switch views from the sidebar, including with Ctrl+click and Alt+click.
- Open Calendar in a narrow split pane and check nothing overlaps.
- Write a long note. Check the word count and saved state under the page, and that the toolbar
  appears when you point at the page and when you type.
- Turn focus mode on and off with each method. Check the sidebar and right panel come back exactly
  as you left them, including when one was already hidden.
- Step through days with the arrows, the keyboard and the month calendar. Try stepping far into the
  past and future.

## Known issues

- On some Windows PCs, **Ctrl+Alt+arrow** is taken by the graphics driver (it rotates the screen)
  or by the keyboard layout (Ctrl+Alt acts as AltGr). If the shortcut doesn't move the day, use the
  ‹ › buttons or the month calendar.
- The Projects list hasn't been redesigned yet. Its count, filters and **New project** are still on
  their own row under the heading.
