# 1.0.0-beta.6 — The mobile app, rebuilt around tabs

The mobile app has a new shape. The four-button bar is gone: every screen is a tab now, as on the
desktop, with a bar of **Search · New · Tabs** at the bottom and a drawer on each side.

**This is a preview.** It has been checked in the desktop's mobile preview, not yet on a phone.
Nothing here changes the desktop app, apart from two small things listed at the end.

Full guide: **Help → The mobile app**.

## What's new

### Tabs, without a tab strip

- **Every screen opens as a tab.** There is no strip to show them; the **Tabs** button at the
  bottom right carries the count and opens the tab switcher.
- **Swipe along the bottom bar to change tabs** — right for the previous one, left for the next.
  It works anywhere on the bar, buttons included, and a swipe never presses the button under it.
- **Each tab keeps its own history.** Swipe in from the left edge of the screen to go back.
  There is no back button.
- Opening the app starts you on **Today** with one tab. Your tabs are not remembered between runs.

### The bottom bar

- **Search** opens search and the command palette. It finds notes and tasks, and lists your other
  open tabs first.
- **New** makes a blank note and opens it in a new tab.
- **Tabs** opens the switcher: a row of pages, one per tab. Tap a page to switch to it, flick it
  up or tap `×` to close it. **Close others** keeps only the tab you are on. While the switcher is
  open the bar reads **Find · New · Done**, and **Find** filters the pages by title.
- Closing the last tab opens Today.

### The left drawer

- The button at the top left opens the same rail as the desktop: the **Notes** and **Planner**
  switch, your note explorer, and **Help**, **Report a bug** and **Settings** at the foot.
- **Tap** a row to open it in the tab you are on. **Press and hold** a row for its menu, which
  starts with **Open** and **Open in new tab** and then has everything a right-click has on the
  desktop.
- A row that is already open in another tab is marked `OPEN`. Tapping it switches to that tab.
- Under **Planner** the drawer lists **List**, **Calendar**, **Habits** and **Projects**.

### The right drawer

- The button at the top right opens it, or swipe in from the right edge.
- On a note it has two tabs. **Tasks** is the day's tasks and habits, with a box to add one that
  is linked to the note. **Properties** has the outline, the folder, the project, tags and links.
- On the **List** it shows the task you last opened under **Tasks**, and the filter under
  **Properties**.
- On **Today** it shows the day's numbers.
- It remembers which tab you left it on.

### While you are typing

- When the cursor is in a note, the bottom bar becomes **B · I · ☐ · # · Done**. The middle of it
  shows which tab you are on, and the swipe still works.
- **Done** puts the keyboard away.

### The `⋯` button

- Every screen has a `⋯` beside its heading. On a note it offers **Rename**, **Move to**,
  **Outline** and **Share**. Everywhere it offers **Forward** when there is somewhere to go
  forward to, and **Close tab** when more than one is open.

### Fixed

- Leaving a note used to land on a page that said *This screen arrives in a later mobile phase*.
- Search was unreachable on the phone: there was nothing on screen that opened it.
- On Android the system back button closed the app. It now closes what is on top, then goes back
  through the tab's history, then closes the tab, and only then leaves the app.

### On the desktop

- Right-click menus are unchanged. The same menus appear as a sheet from the bottom of the screen
  on the phone.
- No other desktop behaviour changes.

## What to test

If you are testing on the desktop, turn on **Settings → Debug → Mobile shell preview**.

1. **The desktop should be exactly as it was.** Tabs, the split, the rail, right-click menus and
   the note sidebar. This release touched shared code to reach the phone, so a desktop regression
   is the most useful thing you could find.
2. **Notes still save.** Type in a note on the phone, switch tabs with a swipe, come back. Then
   close the app, reopen it, and find the note again.
3. **The bar.** Swipe it in both directions from different starting points, including from on top
   of a button. Tap each button. A swipe should never press a button, and a tap should never
   start a swipe.
4. **The tab switcher.** Open several notes in new tabs, open the switcher, and scroll through
   the pages. Flick one up. Close the last one and check you land on Today.
5. **Press and hold.** In the left drawer, hold a note, a folder and a Planner row. Each should
   open a menu, and lifting your finger should not also open the row. Scrolling the list should
   never open a menu.
6. **Back.** Open a note, follow a link from it to another note, and swipe in from the left edge.
   On Android, press the system back button with a drawer open, then with nothing open.
7. **The keyboard.** Put the cursor in a note. The format bar should sit directly above the
   keyboard with no gap and nothing hidden behind it. Tap **B** and check the keyboard stays up.
8. **The right drawer.** On a note, add a task from it and tick one off. Switch to **Properties**,
   close the drawer, open a different note, and check it opens on **Properties**.

## Known issues

- **Not yet tested on a phone.** Everything to do with the on-screen keyboard and with swipes
  from the screen edge has only run in the desktop preview, where neither really exists.
- **Calendar and Projects have no mobile screen.** Their rows in the drawer lead to a placeholder.
- **The pages in the tab switcher are cards, not pictures of the page.** They show the title and
  the kind of page.
- **Tabs cannot be reordered** on the phone yet.
- **Note templates are not in the drawer.** There is no template editor on the phone.
- **Notes cannot be dragged** between folders on the phone. Use **Move to** in the `⋯` menu.
- **Tapping a heading in the outline scrolls the note behind the drawer.** Close the drawer to
  see where it went.
- **On Android, the back button does not close search.** It acts on what is behind it. Tap
  outside search to close it.
- **New note in search opens the capture box**, where the bar's **New** opens a blank note in a
  tab.
- Habits on the phone are still read-only.
