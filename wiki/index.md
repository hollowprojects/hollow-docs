# Hollow — user guide

Hollow is a desktop notebook that keeps your notes, tasks, habits and projects in one place and
syncs them between your devices when you want it to. It works fully offline. This guide covers the
desktop app as it ships in the beta; the phone has [a page of its own](mobile.md).

Everything here is plain Markdown with no screenshots, so it stays accurate as the interface moves.
Where the guide names a menu item or button, it uses the exact label you will see on screen.

The same pages are built into the app: press `F1` or click the **?** at the foot of the sidebar. **Help → What's
new** shows the release notes for the version you are running, and every page ends with **Report an
issue with this page**.

## The shape of the app

- **Left sidebar.** **Today**, **List**, **Calendar** and **Habits** at the top, then your
  **Projects** and your **Notes**, all on show at once, with Help, Report a bug, Settings, the
  sync status and your account at the foot. Click a row to open it; `Ctrl+Shift+T` goes to Today
  from anywhere. It can be full, a narrow column of icons, or hidden.
  See [The sidebar](sidebar.md).
- **The main area is tabbed, and holds up to four panes.** Every screen opens as a tab. Panes can
  sit side by side, stacked, three together or two by two, and can show the same note twice as
  two views of one note. Each pane's tab strip ends in three buttons of its own: new tab, split,
  and a **⋯** menu for the pane. See [Tabs and the split view](tabs-and-split.md).
- **Top strip.** History back and forward and the sidebar button (full, icons, hidden); a
  **Search…** box in the middle that opens the command palette; and, at the right, one control —
  the **Layout** button. It names the saved layout you are in; `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+6` open your
  saved layouts, and four come with Hollow (see
  [Saved layouts](tabs-and-split.md#saved-layouts)).
- **Companions.** There is no right sidebar. What goes beside a screen opens as a tab of its own
  — a **companion** — in the pane next to it: the **Day** (today's habits and tasks, with a box to
  add more) beside a note, a note's **Properties**, the **Task** you clicked in the List, Calendar
  or a project's Tasks tab, and the **Habit** you clicked in Habits. `Ctrl+Shift+\`, or
  **Show companion** / **Hide companion** in a pane's **⋯** menu, shows and hides it, and Hollow
  remembers whether you left it open. See
  [Tabs and the split view](tabs-and-split.md#companions).
- **Settings** live behind the gear at the foot of the sidebar or `Ctrl+,` (`⌘,` on a Mac).
  Shortcuts in this guide are written for Windows; on a Mac read `Ctrl` as `⌘`.

## Vocabulary

- **Task**: something to do once. It has an optional **do date** (when you plan to work on it) and
  an optional **due date** (the deadline). These are different on purpose.
- **Late**: a task whose due date has passed and which you have not rescheduled to a later do date.
  The word means the same thing everywhere in the app.
- **Habit**: something you do on a cadence. Habits never show as Late; they show as done or not
  done on each day they occur.
- **Project**: a container for related notes and tasks, with a status of active, on ice or done.
- **Daily note**: the note for a given day. Today's is pinned as the Notes home screen.
