# Getting started

## Install

The beta ships for Windows as two files. Pick one.

- **MSI installer** — double-click it. It installs for your user only, so no administrator prompt,
  into your local programs folder and adds a Start menu entry named **Hollow Beta**. Uninstall
  from Windows Settings like any other app.
- **Zip** — extract it anywhere and run **Hollow Beta.exe** from the folder. Nothing is installed;
  to remove it, delete the folder.

Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown, because the beta is not code-signed. Choose
**More info → Run anyway**.

Either way, your data is kept separately from the app in your roaming profile folder, so replacing
the app never touches your notes.

## First launch

The first screen asks how you want to use the app.

- Enter your email and password and choose **Log in** to use an existing account and sync with
  your other devices. **Forgot password?** is underneath.
- **Create an account** to start syncing from a new account.
- **Use this device only for now** to skip accounts entirely. Everything stays on this computer.
  You can sign in later and bring all of it with you (see [Sync and your account](sync-and-account.md)).

Choosing the local option takes you straight to today's note. That is the whole setup.

If you have signed in on this computer before, the local option reads **Continue offline**
instead: it opens your synced notes and tasks without signing in again, and uploads your changes
once you are signed in and online.

## Your first minute

- The sidebar on the left has **Today**, **List**, **Calendar** and **Habits** at the top, then
  your **Projects** and your **Notes**. You start on today's daily note. See
  [The sidebar](sidebar.md).
- Type in the page. It saves as you go; the line under the text shows the word count and whether
  your changes are saved.
- Press `N` anywhere outside a text field to capture a task. Type a title, optionally a date,
  and press Enter. It lands in the Planner.
- Press `Ctrl+K` to open the command palette and type what you are looking for or what you want
  to do.
- For step-by-step recipes for the things you will do every day, see [Workflows](workflows.md).

## Updating

Updates are manual during the beta.

- MSI: run the new installer over the old one. Your data folder is untouched.
- Zip: delete the old folder and extract the new one.

Before installing an update that changes how data is stored, export a backup first
(see [Backup and your data](backup-and-data.md)). The version and build you are running are
shown in **Settings → About**.

## Where things are

| Thing | Where |
|---|---|
| Notes, tasks, habits, projects | The sidebar: the **List**, **Calendar** and **Habits** rows, and the **Projects** and **Notes** sections |
| Today at a glance | **Today** at the top of the sidebar opens the Today dashboard |
| Today's note | `Ctrl+Shift+T`, or **Today's page** when you right-click **Today** |
| Task list and calendar | **List** and **Calendar** in the sidebar |
| Habits | **Habits** in the sidebar |
| Projects | The **Projects** section lists the active ones; click its heading, or **All projects** at its foot, for all of them (can be hidden in **Settings → Desk → Show Projects**) |
| Whether sync is working | The dot and word at the foot of the sidebar, when you are signed in |
| Search and actions | **Search…** in the top strip, or `Ctrl+K` |
| Settings | The gear at the foot of the sidebar, or `Ctrl+,` |
| This guide, inside the app | The **?** at the foot of the sidebar, or `F1` |
| What changed in this version | **Help → What's new**, or the palette |
| Report a bug | The bug at the foot of the sidebar, or `Ctrl+Shift+B` |

## Help is built in

Everything in this guide is also inside the app: press `F1` or click the **?** at the foot of the
sidebar. The **What's new** page there lists what changed in the version you are running. Every Help page ends
with **Report an issue with this page**, which opens the bug reporter pre-filled with the page
name, so if something in the guide is wrong or unclear you can say so from where you read it.
