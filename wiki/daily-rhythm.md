# Daily rhythm

Hollow is built around landing on today and leaving it deliberately.

## Landing: today's note and the glance

Every launch opens today's daily note. Beside it, unless you closed it last time, is the **Day**
— the day glance, a tab of its own taking about a quarter of the width. Its header reads **Day**
and the date (*Today · Monday, Sep 28*), with **List** and **Calendar** buttons that open those in
a new tab. If it isn't showing, press `Ctrl+Shift+1`, or right-click **Today** in the sidebar (or
the Today tab) → **Day to the side**.

- A quick-add input at the top (**Add a task, or /note · /habit**). Tasks you add here are planned for that day (their do date) and
  linked to the note. Start the line with `/note` to make a note or `/habit` to make a habit
  instead.
- Your **habits** for the day with checkboxes. A habit with steps shows a count and a chevron to
  tick the steps off.
- Tasks **due today** and tasks **planned for today**, with anything late on top: a red check and
  a small **Nd late** marker.
- A task with checklist subtasks shows a count such as `2/5` and a chevron that opens them.
- Click a task or habit to open its editor right there in the Day; **‹ back** returns to the
  day's tasks, and **open in Planner ›** takes it to the Planner.
- Right-click a task for **Schedule** (the seven days from the day shown, as the do date), **Unschedule**, **Show in Calendar
  to the side** (on this day, every project) and **Show in List below**.

The glance keeps a half-typed entry if you switch notes and come back.

## The Today dashboard

**Today** at the top of the sidebar opens the dashboard: today on one screen. Each tile is a
summary; click its heading to open the real screen.

- **Add a task for today** at the top: type and press Enter. The task is planned for today.
- **Today** — what is due or planned today, first five, with a checkbox to tick each off. Click a
  title to open the task beside the dashboard; click the heading for the List on its **Today**
  filter.
- **Next** — the next tasks with a time today.
- **Overdue** — how many, and the oldest three. The heading opens the List's **Pool**.
- **Habits** — today's habits; click one to tick it off.
- **Projects this week** — active projects with tasks overdue or due in the next seven days.
- **Journal** — the first lines of today's page, or **Start today's page**. Click to open it.
- **Next seven days** — a bar for each day, as tall as its share of the week's tasks. Click a day
  to open the Calendar on it.

The dashboard always shows today, whichever day the journal is on. It is the phone's Today screen
on a phone.

## Looking back and ahead

The header of a daily note reads **‹**, the date, **›**, then **Today**. Use the arrows, or
`Ctrl+Alt+←` / `Ctrl+Alt+→`, to step a day at a time, or click the date for a month calendar with
a dot on every day you wrote. A day you never wrote shows the journal's template in grey (or
"Nothing written on this day." without one) and still shows its tasks in the Day beside it;
nothing is created unless you click the page. The Day
follows the day you are viewing on Today; beside any other note it shows today. The **Today**
button brings you back. `Ctrl+Shift+T` goes to the Today tab; pressed again there,
it jumps to today's date.

On past days the glance is read-only: you can look, but not tick.

On some Windows machines `Ctrl+Alt+Arrow` belongs to the graphics driver (it rotates the screen)
or types a character on keyboards with an AltGr key. If the shortcut does nothing for you, the
arrows and the calendar do the same thing.

## Leaving: sign off for the day

Right-click **Today** at the top of the sidebar, or open the command palette, and choose **Sign
off for today**. A sheet lists today's habits, then
everything still open for today under **Still open**, late items first.

For each task:

- Tick its **check** to complete it.
- **Tomorrow** moves its do date to tomorrow.
- **Pick day** opens the date menu: the next seven days, **Next week** with no day, **Pick a
  date…** or **No date**.
- **Park** sends it back to the Pool as Backlog, with no do date.

Tick any habits you kept. Click **Done for today**, or close the sheet, when you are finished.

Sign-off changes **do dates** only. Moving a late task to tomorrow answers it — it stops counting
as late — but its due date stays where it was. If the deadline itself moved, change the due date
in the task's detail or the List.

## Planning the week

- **Planner → Calendar → Week** shows the week, due tasks on their days. Click **Pool** in its
  header to open the tasks with no day beside it, then drag them onto a day to make them due then,
  or onto an hour to give them a time.
- To plan a task for a day without giving it a deadline, set its **do date** — it shows in the
  Calendar with a dashed outline. **Next week** in a do-date menu plans it for next week with no
  day; it waits in the Pool, labelled *next week*.
- **Agenda** lists the next four weeks without a grid, skipping empty days.
- The **List**'s date groups — Overdue, Today, Next 7 days, Later, Unscheduled — put each task on
  the same day as the Calendar.

## Rescheduling

- On a **List** row, the date cell's menu moves the due date: the next seven days, **Time…**,
  **Pick a date…** and **No date**. For a task with only a do date it moves the do date instead.
- In the **Calendar**, drag the task, use **Alt+arrows**, or its **⋯ → Pick a date…**.
- In the **sign-off** sheet, **Tomorrow** and **Pick day** move the do date.
- The task's detail has both dates under **More details…**.

Giving a late task a do date of today or later clears Late everywhere at once.
